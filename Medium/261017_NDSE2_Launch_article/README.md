## Publish on Medium

1. Use the article title and italic subtitle as Medium title and subtitle.
2. Upload the banner as the first image, set as the story's featured image.
3. Paste the article paragraphs into Medium and upload the images individually at the marked positions: Medium does not automatically resolve relative Markdown image paths.
4. Check captions beneath figures and maintain links to the primary studies.
5. The article references the book and the series using exactly the same Amazon address supplied by the author: `https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0HN9KQLNG`. Replace the *series* link in future if Amazon provides a distinct series page.
